## False clauses / traps

1. **Draft A `velocityDifference_support` is FALSE as stated.** `tsupport (fun x => velocity ε (t,x) − v (t,x)) ⊆ Metric.ball place.x₀ r`. The difference `u_ε − v = w_ε + U_ε` is a sum of **unit-periodic** fields, so whenever it is not identically zero on a time slice (which the blow-up requires near `T`), its `tsupport` in `ℝ³ = Space` contains `x₀ + ℤ³`-translates and is therefore **never** contained in one bounded ball. Counterexample: at any `t ∈ (T−ε², T)` on which `U_ε ≠ 0`, `U_ε(t, x₀ + e₁) = U_ε(t, x₀) ≠ 0` (unit period), and `x₀ + e₁ ∉ ball x₀ r` for `r < 1`. This is exactly the logged lesson from T24 ("`tsupport` of a periodized field is never inside one ball"). A also silently drops the paper's "diameter `O(ε)`". Draft B's `periodicSet (ball x₀ (ε·ρ))` + `diffSupport_in_chart` is the correct form.
2. **Draft B is not provable from its own parameters** (not a falsity, a provability gap): with `P : PacketAPI` (no energy predicate) and no `ScalingAPI` parameter, `energyRate`/`forceDifference_mixed_bound`/`forceDifference_sobolev_bound` cannot be discharged — the torus scaling identities are unavailable. Fixed by the packet+scaling ruling.
3. **Draft B mixed guard is insufficient** (not a falsity): `forceDifference_spatial_memLp` (per-slice `MemLp`) does not prevent the `mixedLebesgueENormT = ⨅ = ⊤` trap; replace with `MemMixedLebesgueT`.

No clause of the theorem is **missing from both** drafts: every display of `:287-306` (clauses (i)–(iv), `eq:insertion`, the two cross-transport identities, the `s<0` tail) is present in at least one; the union, after the rulings, covers all. (A alone omits the `O(ε)` diameter and the ess-sup `limsup=∞`; B alone omits — correctly — the extra T11/T12/breakdown apparatus.)
