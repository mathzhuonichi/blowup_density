## False clauses / traps

1. **Draft A `difference_support_interior` / `velocityDifference_support` are FALSE as stated.** The support ball is centered at the **origin**: `velocityDifference_support : tsupport(u_ε(t,·)−v(t,·)) ⊆ ball (0:Space) (ε·ρ)` and `difference_support_interior : ball (0:Space) (ε·ρ) ⊆ interior_region`, with `interior_region_subset : interior_region ⊆ interior Ω` and `difference_support_radius_pos : 0 < ρ`. Together these force `ball 0 (ε·ρ) ⊆ interior Ω`, i.e. `0 ∈ interior Ω`. **Counterexample:** `Ω = (1,2)³` is a bounded box (`IsBoundedBoxOrSmoothDomain`), and the reference hypotheses of `boundaryInsertionStatement` are satisfiable there; but `0 ∉ interior Ω`, so no `BoundaryInsertionAPI` instance exists — `boundaryInsertionStatement` (A) is FALSE. Root cause: hard-coding the insertion at the origin instead of the paper's *arbitrary prescribed interior ball* (`:646`). (Independently, blow-up must occur inside `Ω` via `DomainSpeedUnboundedAt Ω`, which the origin-centered support also contradicts.)
2. **Draft B is VACUOUS for `Ω` disjoint from the unit cube** (fidelity trap, not a falsity). B threads `place : PlacementData P.toPacketAPI`, which carries the **torus artifact** `chartBall_in_cube : closure(ball chartCenter chartRadius) ⊆ interior fundamentalCube = (0,1)³`. `boundaryInsertionStatement` (B) additionally hypothesizes `closure(ball chartCenter chartRadius) ⊆ Ω`. For `Ω` with `Ω ∩ (0,1)³ = ∅` (e.g. `Ω=(1,2)³`) no `place` satisfies both, so the statement is **vacuously true** and says nothing — yet `cor:boundary` claims the insertion for *every* bounded box/smooth domain. `fundamentalCube` is meaningless on a general `Ω` (there is no torus). **Fix (shared root with #1):** the interior placement must be a **cube-free** free center `x₀` with `closure(ball x₀ r) ⊆ Ω`; drop `chartBall_in_cube`. See §3–§4.
5. **No clause of `cor:boundary` is missing from both drafts.** Every display of `:632-666` — the box/smooth domain, the assumed reference on `Ω` with no-slip and gauge, the three insertion displays, the interior-ball construction, momentum-unchanged + cross-transport, the collar⇒no-slip, `L²(Ω)`/`L¹H^s(Ω)` estimates, the `s<0` tail, the fixed-`K` post-`T` support, the `eq:zero-extension` two-sided `ε`-uniform comparison, no-slip uniqueness, and singular-exactly-at-`T` — is present in at least one; the union, after the rulings, covers all.
