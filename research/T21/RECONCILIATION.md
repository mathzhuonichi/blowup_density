## False clauses / traps

1. **Draft A's T20 copy occupies the canonical namespace.** `DraftA.lean:818,938` declares `structure CriticalRegularityTAPI` inside `namespace NSFormalization.Section3.T20` — the exact fully-qualified name of the canonical module's record (`Section3/T20/CriticalRegularity.lean:139`). Draft B recorded this; it is restated here because the reconciled file keeps B's indexing.

Positive checks performed on the retained fields (none is a tautology, none is vacuous):
`hc`, `thresholdValue` are concrete real (in)equalities; `zeroInitialClass`, `zeroMemBall` unfold to the registered smoothness/periodicity/solenoidality/support predicates; `sliceSobolevMonotone` and `forceSobolevMonotone` compare two `⨅`-with-`⊤` extended norms (a missing representative gives `⊤`, never a junk `0`), and both are true (`periodicFrequencyWeight k = 1 + 4π²|k|² ≥ 1`, so the order-lowering weight is `≤ 1`); `ballRelativelyOpen` quantifies the radius inside the ball point, so it is the honest "every point is interior" and needs the triangle inequality; `criticalBallDisjoint`/`ballDisjoint` are `Disjoint` between sets one of which is shown inhabited by `zeroMemBall`; `nonDensity`/`zeroInitialNonDensity` negate the registered positive-radius density predicate.
