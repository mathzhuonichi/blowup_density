## 1. What is registered

`T01.torus_data`, version 1, registers the T10 periodic data layer on the unit
three-torus.  Its contract vocabulary is exactly the definitions in
`PeriodicData.lean` through `IsPeriodicReweight`, plus `IsPeriodicOn`.  The
five TorusCube declarations are contract-side restatements, not allowlist
extensions.  Lead amendment 1 is included in both datum predicates and in the
forward Parseval hypothesis.

6. `torusLift_surjective`
7. `mean_decomposition`
8. `meanZero_datum`
9. `leray_exists_contraction`

Every restated declaration has an `rfl` drift guard.  `PeriodicSobolev s` is
bridged through `realPeriodicSubmodule`; the polymorphic periodicity/lift
bridges are pointwise because their bare equalities leave implicit parameters
underconstrained.  `checkedTorusData` has only `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
and `Quot.sound` transitively.

## 3. Gaps and exact error text

The solution-class part is intentionally deferred to T11:
`IsPeriodicSobolevPath`, force norms/classes, pressure normalization/gauge,
`ClassicalSolutionT` and its fieldwise structure conversions, lifespan,
breakdown/density, and physical/coefficient energy norms are absent from this
contract.

The requested direct four-proof-module binding is impossible in the current
canonical source because three modules give anonymous local torus-measure
instances the same generated global names.  The first exact error was:


The ground rules prohibited repairing those `formalization/` modules.  The
binding imports compatible `DatumBasics + Leray`, directly uses their five
named API theorems, and repeats the proved `Parseval`/`PhysicalBridge`
arguments privately for the remaining five fields.  This adds no assumption;
the axiom audit is green.  Full pairwise failures and rationale are in
`ATTEMPTS_CONTRACT.md`.
