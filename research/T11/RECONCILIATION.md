(iv) the criterion as an `ℝ≥0∞` `lintegral` of `periodicSobolevENorm 2` of the **full (inhomogeneous, mean included)** velocity over `Ioo 0 S`, finiteness spelled `≠ ⊤`, with the same reason (a real Bochner integral is junk-`0` on a non-integrable integrand); (vi) `restart` must return a solution for `timeShiftT t₀ f` **directly** rather than through `horizon`, because a positive time shift of an `F_T` force need not vanish at its new time zero, so `timeShiftT t₀ f ∉ forceClassT` — this is a deliberate, independently-reached divergence from `RestartFixedForce`;
